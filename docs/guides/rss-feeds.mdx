---
title: "RSS feeds"
description: "Discover and scrape new stories from RSS feeds."
---

1. Open the **RSS Feeds** tab and click **Add RSS Feed**.
2. Check the feed to record new items. Each item is stored as unscraped.
3. Click **Scrape New Items** to scrape a batch of unscraped items into your library.

Feeds are checked when you ask; there is no automatic schedule yet. See the [RSS API](/api-reference/rss).
