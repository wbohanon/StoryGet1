---
title: "Collections"
description: "Organize stories by genre, author, series, or anything else."
---

Collections group stories. A story can belong to several collections, and each collection has a name, an optional description, and a color (default `#4a6fa5`).

In the web UI:

1. Open the **Collections** tab.
2. Click **Create Collection**.
3. Add stories to it from the story viewer.

Deleting a collection does not delete its stories. See the [collections API](/api-reference/collections).
